PAIL:Vision-Guided Predictive Action Imitation Learning with Discrete Latent Encoding for Multitasking Robots

Sai Navaneet, Lingala Manisha, SangMoon Lee
Kyungpook National University
Paper arXiv Video Code Data

Abstract

This paper presents Vision-Guided Predictive Action Imitation Learning with Discrete Latent Encoding for Multitasking Robots (PAIL), a novel approach designed to overcome critical challenges in imitation learning. Unlike traditional methods that rely on Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), PAIL employs discrete latent representations to enhance efficiency and reduce memory usage, while maintaining high-quality data encoding. By leveraging quantized embeddings, PAIL avoids redundant continuous optimization, making it more resource-efficient. Additionally, PAIL incorporates a Transformer-based architecture with cross-attention mechanisms, enabling it to capture long-range dependencies effectively in sequential decision-making tasks. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that PAIL surpasses traditional VAE-based methods, showcasing its potential for scalable and resource-efficient robotic learning.
